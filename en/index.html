How a visit works

  1. Send a photo

    DM a photo of your brows without makeup. We tell you whether grooming is enough or a tattoo is needed.

  2. Pick a date

    Brows look darker on days 2–4. Leave 4–5 days before important photos or events.

  3. About 60 minutes

    Consultation, a design drawn on your face, a soothing cream, then the procedure. The director does every step.

  4. Aftercare

    Keep water and sweat off the brows for two weeks. The final result shows at day 28.

FAQ

Questions from clients abroad

Do you speak English?
Enough for a consultation. Send a photo of your brows by Instagram DM first and we will tell you the direction before you book.
I cannot come back for a touch-up. Is one visit enough?
Yes. For clients who live abroad we adjust density and depth so the result settles in one session, and we design one shade lighter so it never looks drawn on.
How long does it take and does it hurt?
About 60 minutes including consultation and design. A soothing cream is applied for 30 minutes before we start; most people describe it as a light prick.
How long does it last?
Usually one to one and a half years, fading gradually.
How do I care for it while travelling?
Keep water and sweat off the brows for two weeks, do not pick the flakes, and expect the colour to look dark on days 2–4 and light on days 8–14. The final result shows at day 28.
How much is it?
Fixed pricing with one touch-up included. Ask by Instagram DM for the current price.
How do I get there?
3F, 12-32 Dongseongno 2-gil, Jung-gu, Daegu. Five minutes on foot from Banwoldang Station exit 13. No parking in the building; paid parking lots nearby.

Aftercare

What to expect after an eyebrow tattoo in Korea: darker on days 2–4, flaking on days 5–7, lighter on days 8–14, final at day 28. Water, sweat, sauna and sun rules for travellers.
