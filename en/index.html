FAQ

Questions from clients abroad

Do you speak English?
Enough for a consultation. Send a photo of your brows by Instagram DM first and we will tell you the direction before you book.
I cannot come back for a touch-up. Is one visit enough?
Yes. For clients who live abroad we adjust density and depth so the result settles in one session, and we design one shade lighter so it never looks drawn on.
How long does it take and does it hurt?
About 60 minutes including consultation and design. A numbing cream is applied for 30 minutes before we start; most people describe it as a light prick.
How do I care for it while travelling?
Keep water and sweat off the brows for two weeks, do not pick the flakes, and expect the colour to look dark on days 2–4 and light on days 8–14. The final result shows at day 28.
How do I get there?
3F, 12-32 Dongseongno 2-gil, Jung-gu, Daegu. Five minutes on foot from Banwoldang Station exit 13. Parking is available.
