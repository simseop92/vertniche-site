Aftercare

Aftercare while you travel, day 1 to day 28

Aftercare is mostly about what not to do for the first two weeks.

You can wash your face from day one. Keep water and sweat off the brows for two weeks and let the flakes fall on their own.

Instagram DMKakaoTalk

Day 1: washing and showering

You can wash your face and shower on the day. Just keep water off the brows. Makeup is fine around them, but do not fill the brows in.

Apply the regeneration cream we give you once, thinly, on the evening of the procedure. After that, leave the brows alone.

Days 2–4: suddenly darker

This is normal. The pigment oxidises and looks darker than it will end up. It is not the final colour, so do not judge it yet.

Days 5–7: flaking

Let the flakes fall by themselves. Picking them pulls the pigment out and leaves patches. Skip Vaseline and creams in this period; dry is better for the pigment.

Flakes fall unevenly, and some people barely flake at all. Both are normal.

Days 8–14: it looks faded

Also normal. The surface pigment has gone with the flakes and the colour under the skin has not come up yet. It returns from day 15. Do not get another procedure elsewhere in this period.

Sauna, swimming, workouts and sun

No sauna, swimming, hot baths or heavy sweating for two weeks. A walk is fine. You can wash your hair; keep hot air and shampoo water off the brows.

On strong-sun days a hat is enough. Keep peels and face masks off the brow area for one to two weeks.

Day 28: the final result

The final result shows around day 28, when the skin has finished renewing. If you are unsure at any stage, send a photo by Instagram DM and we will tell you where you are.
